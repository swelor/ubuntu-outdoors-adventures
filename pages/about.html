About Ubuntu Outdoor Adventures

Our Story

Founded in 2010 in Johannesburg, Ubuntu Outdoor Adventures began with a simple mission: to share the breathtaking beauty of South Africa's natural landscapes with the world. What started as a small hiking group has grown into one of the country's most trusted outdoor tourism companies.

Our Team

Our team consists of certified mountain guides, experienced campers, and passionate nature enthusiasts who call South Africa's wild places home. Every guide is trained in first aid, conservation, and local ecology.

Why Choose Us

  • Over 15 years of experience in outdoor tourism
  • Fully accredited and safety-certified guides
  • Small group sizes for personalised experiences
  • Commitment to sustainable and responsible tourism
  • Affordable packages for all budgets
